# Stakeholder Matrix
## Corporate Back-Office Administration System

### 1. Purpose

The stakeholder matrix identifies key project stakeholders, their level of influence and interest, responsibilities, communication needs, and engagement approach.

This helps ensure that the appropriate stakeholders receive relevant information throughout the project lifecycle.

---

### 2. Stakeholder Assessment

| Stakeholder | Role | Influence | Interest | Key Responsibility | Engagement Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Sponsor | Executive Sponsor | High | Medium | Project direction and major approvals | Monthly executive update |
| Project Manager | Project Lead | High | High | Overall project delivery | Weekly project review |
| Business Stakeholders | Business Representatives | High | High | Requirements, feedback and UAT | Weekly stakeholder meeting |
| Project Management Analyst | Project Coordination | Medium | High | Tracking, reporting and coordination | Daily project monitoring |
| System Analyst | Requirements & System Analysis | High | High | Requirements, process analysis and documentation | Daily collaboration |
| Developers | Technical Team | Medium | High | System development and technical implementation | Daily Scrum |
| QA Team | Quality Assurance | Medium | High | Test execution and defect validation | Testing status meetings |
| External Vendors | Integration / Deployment Support | Medium | Medium | Technical integration and deployment support | Scheduled coordination meetings |
| End Users | System Users | Low | High | UAT feedback and system validation | UAT sessions and user feedback |

---

### 3. Power / Interest Classification

#### Manage Closely

Stakeholders with high influence and high interest.

- Project Sponsor
- Project Manager
- Business Stakeholders
- System Analyst

These stakeholders should receive frequent updates and be actively involved in important project decisions.

#### Keep Satisfied

Stakeholders with high influence but lower day-to-day interest.

- Executive Management

Provide concise progress updates and escalate major risks, decisions, and issues when required.

#### Keep Informed

Stakeholders with lower influence but high interest.

- End Users
- QA Team
- Developers

Provide relevant project updates and ensure they have the information required to perform their responsibilities.

#### Monitor

Stakeholders with lower influence and lower interest.

- Supporting stakeholders who are not directly involved in project delivery.

Engagement should be maintained when their input or involvement becomes necessary.

---

### 4. Communication Approach

| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Scrum | Developers, QA, System Analyst | Daily | Track progress and identify blockers |
| Project Status Meeting | Project Manager, PM Analyst, Business Stakeholders | Weekly | Review progress, risks and upcoming activities |
| Executive Update | Project Sponsor | Monthly / As Required | Provide high-level project status |
| UAT Session | Business Stakeholders, QA, System Analyst | During UAT | Validate business requirements |
| Deployment Meeting | Technical Team, Vendors, Project Team | Before deployment | Confirm deployment readiness |
| Project Closure Meeting | Key Stakeholders | End of project | Review outcomes and lessons learned |

---

### 5. Stakeholder Engagement Objectives

The project team aims to:

- Maintain clear and consistent communication.
- Ensure stakeholders understand project progress.
- Identify concerns and risks early.
- Obtain timely decisions and approvals.
- Manage expectations regarding scope and timelines.
- Ensure business stakeholders remain engaged throughout UAT.
- Maintain alignment between business and technical teams.
